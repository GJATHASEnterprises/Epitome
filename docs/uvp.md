# Epitome Step — Unique Value Proposition (Sept 2026 planning estimates)

## Positioning

- **Step Walnut ($99):** minimalist, warm desk aesthetic.
- **Step Obsidian ($109):** gamer battlestation aesthetic with RGB glow lines.

Both use the same charging architecture and include a 65W GaN brick, one USB-C input cable, and one USB-C output cable.

## Why it works

1. Distinct buyer fit instead of one compromise design.
2. Premium-looking materials at accessible pricing.
3. Practical charging setup: 20W magnetic phone, up-to-15W second zone, 5W watch zone, and 15W USB-C output.

## Product identity

- Walnut: black PETG base + wood-PLA top, sanded and oiled, single white status LED.
- Obsidian: full CF-PETG shell, WS2812B side glow lines, Digispark-style ATtiny85 USB mode control.

## Compliance and wording guardrails

- Use: "Qi2 magnetic charging — compatible with MagSafe-case iPhones and Qi2 Android phones."
- Do not use bare "MagSafe" as a product claim.
- Watch-zone decision remains open: MFi module, explicit non-Apple-Watch positioning, or remove watch zone.
