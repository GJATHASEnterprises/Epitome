# Epitome Step — Unique Value Proposition (Sept 2026)

## Positioning

- **Step Walnut ($99):** minimalist, warm desk aesthetic.
- **Step Obsidian ($109):** gamer battlestation aesthetic with RGB glow lines.

Both use the same charging architecture and include a 65W GaN brick + cable.

## Why it works

1. Distinct buyer fit instead of one compromise design.
2. Premium-looking materials at accessible pricing.
3. Clean setup with customer-paid shipping and transparent checkout economics.

## Product identity

- Walnut: black PETG base + wood-PLA top, sanded and oiled, single white status LED.
- Obsidian: full CF-PETG shell, WS2812B side glow lines, ESP32-C3 mode control.
