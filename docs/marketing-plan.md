# Epitome — Marketing Plan (Batch 1)

**Budget: ~$600 paid marketing** (trimmed from $1,000 after Step Go reserve). Rule: **no paid spend until** real photos exist, listing is complete, and 1–2 organic sales prove conversion.

## Sequence

1. Organic posts (free) → 2. Etsy Ads → 3. Gifted units → 4. Meta/IG → 5. Scale the winner

## Channel allocation

| Channel | Budget | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Etsy Ads (Walnut) | $150 | $3–5/day × 30 days. Kill at $75 spend with zero sales. |
| Meta/Instagram ads | $250 | $10/day min. 15-sec vertical video: devices placed one-by-one, LEDs glow, "Charge everything. Touch nothing." |
| Reddit Ads test (Obsidian) | $50 | r/battlestations, r/pcmasterrace. Maker-post tone only. |
| 2 gifted units → micro desk-setup influencers (10–50K) | ~$90 | Best ROI in niche; generates reusable ad creative. |
| Photo props/backdrop | $50 | Phone camera + window light is fine. |
| **Reserve** | remainder | Double down on whichever channel converts. |

## Etsy listing requirements (before ads)

- Photo 1 = lifestyle hero (walnut on warm desk, devices charging)
- All 10 photo slots + a 5–15 sec video
- Title: "Walnut Wireless Charging Station 3-in-1 — Handmade Wood Charger for Phone, Watch, Earbuds"
- All 13 tags: wood wireless charger, walnut desk accessory, charging station, nightstand organizer, gift for him, tech gift, 3 in 1 charger, minimalist desk, etc.

## Expected economics

- Etsy CPC $0.20–0.50; @2% conversion ≈ $10–25 CAC on $99 sale (~$45 margin post-redesign) → profitable
- Meta cold-traffic CAC $20–40 → survivable on redesigned margins only
- Honest specs in copy: "compatible with Qi-enabled devices," "65W brick, no brownouts, ever." Never "MagSafe," never "Qi2 certified."

## Do NOT spend on

- Google Ads (keyword costs $2–5/click vs Anker)
- Paid influencers (gift units instead)
- TikTok ads (post organic maker content instead)
