# Step — Finishing Guide

---

## Walnut Surface Finishing

### Materials
- Rubio Monocoat oil (shared across batch, ÷20 units cost)
- Lint-free cloths ×3 per unit (application, wipe, buff)
- 120 / 180 / 220 grit sandpaper
- Tack cloth

### Process

1. **Sand** all walnut pieces through 120 → 180 → 220 grit along grain direction
2. **Blow off** dust with compressed air
3. **Wipe** with tack cloth — no dust should remain
4. **Apply oil** — dip lint-free cloth, apply very thin coat, work into grain
5. **Wipe off** excess oil within 3 minutes — do not leave wet
6. **Buff** with a clean dry cloth until no residue
7. **Cure** — allow minimum 1 hour before handling, 24 hours before assembly
8. **Do not** apply heat or water within 24 hours

### Notes
- Use less oil than you think you need — Rubio is one-coat, not multi-coat
- Grain should run along the long dimension of each piece
- Do not oil the mating/gluing surfaces (edges that receive wood glue)
- Oil one batch of pieces at a time; pieces may be stacked after 1 hour cure

---

## ABS Base Finishing

### Post-Print
1. Remove supports
2. Sand seam lines: 120 → 180 grit wet
3. Acetone smoothing: wipe with acetone-damp cloth for matte surface
4. Final 220 grit dry sand to uniform matte
5. Wipe clean — no sanding residue before electronics installation

### Notes
- Work in ventilated area for acetone step
- Acetone smoothing is quick — one wipe, do not over-apply
- Target finish: uniform low-sheen matte black, no visible layer lines

---

## Silicone Pad Installation

1. Cut silicone sheet to size for each zone:
   - Zone 1: 75×90mm portrait
   - Zone 2: 65×50mm
   - Zone 3: 55×55mm
2. Test fit in recesses before bonding
3. Apply thin bead of silicone adhesive or RTV to recess edges
4. Press silicone pad into recess, align flat
5. Allow adhesive to cure per manufacturer instructions before first use

---

## LED Diffuser Installation

1. Cut frosted acrylic to 130×10mm if not pre-cut
2. Test fit in front fascia slot
3. Secure with a thin bead of acrylic cement or RTV on edges
4. Do not cover LED strip — diffuser sits in front of LEDs, not on them
