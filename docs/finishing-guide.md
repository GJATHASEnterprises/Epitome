# Epitome Penta — 3D Print Finishing Guide

> This guide covers finishing for all ABS parts — both the 3D printed centre platform and the laser cut slot walls, base, and top panels.

All centre platforms are 3D printed in ABS and require finishing before assembly. This guide covers the full process to achieve a professional matte black surface indistinguishable from injection moulded at normal viewing distance.

## Step 1 — Sanding

Do this immediately after removing the print from the bed and removing supports.

1. **120 grit** — remove worst layer lines and any support marks. 5–10 min per part.
2. **240 grit** — smooth further. 5 min.
3. **400 grit wet sand** — use water as lubricant, circular motions. 5 min.
4. **800 grit wet sand** — final pre-acetone surface. 3 min.

Wipe dry with a clean cloth between grits.

## Step 2 — Acetone vapour smoothing

⚠️ Do this OUTSIDE or with all windows open. Acetone vapour is flammable. No open flames nearby.

1. Pour 2–3 tablespoons of acetone into a small dish. Place dish at bottom of large plastic storage bin.
2. Suspend the part on a wire rack or hang it on a hook ABOVE the liquid — part must NOT touch the acetone.
3. Seal the lid tightly.
4. Wait 15–20 minutes. Do not open early.
5. Remove part carefully. Surface will look glossy and smooth — layer lines largely dissolved.
6. Air dry on a clean surface for 30 minutes minimum before touching.

Result: surface appears near-injection-moulded.

## Step 3 — Primer

1. Light 400 grit wet sand after acetone drying — removes any sag or drips from vapour.
2. Apply Rust-Oleum Filler Primer in 2 light coats, 15 minutes apart.
3. Let dry 1 hour.
4. Lightly wet sand with 600 grit.
5. 1 final coat of primer.
6. Let dry fully — 2 hours minimum.

## Step 4 — Paint

1. Apply Rust-Oleum Matte Black in 3 thin coats. 15 minutes between coats.
2. Do not apply thick coats — runs are hard to fix.
3. Let final coat dry 1 hour.

## Step 5 — Clear coat

1. Apply Matte Clear Coat in 2 light coats.
2. 15 minutes between coats.
3. Let cure fully — 24 hours before handling in assembly.

## Time per part

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Sanding | ~20 min |
| Acetone setup + wait | ~45 min (mostly waiting) |
| Primer + dry | ~3 hours (mostly waiting) |
| Paint + dry | ~1.5 hours (mostly waiting) |
| Clear coat + cure | ~24 hours (cure overnight) |

Run all 10 parts through each step together as a batch — acetone all 10 on the same day, prime all 10 the next day, paint all 10 the day after. Total active hands-on time across all 10 parts: ~4 hours.
