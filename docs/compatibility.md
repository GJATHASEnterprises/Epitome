# Epitome Step — Device Compatibility

---

## Zone 1 — Phone (Qi2 magnetic, up to 20W)

| Device | Compatible | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone 13 and newer (with MagSafe-compatible case) | ✅ | Qi2 magnetic alignment |
| Qi2-enabled Android phones | ✅ | Qi2 alignment where supported |
| Other Qi-enabled phones | ✅ | Standard Qi fallback |

---

## Zone 2 — Buds / second phone (plain Qi, up to 15W)

| Device | Compatible | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| AirPods / Galaxy Buds / Pixel Buds (Qi case models) | ✅ | Typical draw is 2–5W |
| Qi-enabled second phone | ✅ | Non-magnetic placement |
| Non-Qi accessories | ❌ | Requires Qi receiver |

---

## Zone 3 — Watch zone

| Device | Compatible | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Generic Qi watch devices | ✅ | With generic coil option |
| Apple Watch | ❌ by default | Requires separate MFi-certified module option |

Do not market Apple Watch support unless the MFi option is selected.

---

## USB-C output (rear)

| Port | Protocol | Compatible devices |
|---|---|---|
| USB-C output (15W) | USB-C PD class output | Phones, earbuds cases, small accessories, speakers |

In-box cables: 1 input cable + 1 output-device cable.

---

## Wording compliance

- Use: "Qi2 magnetic charging — compatible with MagSafe-case iPhones and Qi2 Android phones."
- Use: "up to 60W total."
- Avoid bare "MagSafe" as a standalone product claim.
