## Batch 1 — Standard SKU Pilot (13–15 Units)

### Goal
Launch and validate the Standard SKU (~530mm width), universal compatibility positioning, and captive-cable UX.

### Enclosure Method
- Prototype/short-run enclosure with Standard geometry
- Reinforced Step 2 riser (ribbing/insert)

### Budget

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Electronics + modules + cables | ~$1,480–$1,710 |
| Enclosure | ~$585–$975 |
| Packaging | ~$120–$205 |
| **Total build-only** | **~$2,185–$2,890** |

### Pricing and Revenue

| SKU | Early Bird | Pre-Order | Retail |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Standard | $199 | $249 | $279 |
| XL (future) | $229 | $279 | $309 |

### Break-Even Analysis

Approximate Standard-only break-even: **~10 units** against midpoint build budget.

---

## Batch 2 — Scale + XL Introduction (50 Units)

### Trigger
Start after Standard pilot validation and supply-chain readiness.

### Changes from Batch 1
- Introduce XL SKU (~700mm width, 400mm laptop slot)
- Keep all other geometry and electronics architecture shared with Standard

### Budget (50 Units)

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Components + assembly | ~$3,350–$3,900 |
| Enclosures | ~$1,000–$1,300 |
| Packaging | ~$275–$350 |
| **Total** | **~$4,625–$5,550** |

### Revenue (mixed Standard/XL)
Target blended gross margin remains healthy with MSRP ladder.

## Certification Milestone Details

Required certifications and timing/budget planning:
- **FCC (USA)**
- **CE (EU)**
- **UKCA (UK)**
- **RCM (Australia)**

Estimated per-certification cost: **$2,000–$8,000**
Typical timeline: **8–16 weeks each**

These approvals are mandatory before retail sales in each corresponding market.
