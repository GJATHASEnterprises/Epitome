# Epitome Penta — Production Roadmap

## Batch 1 — Standard SKU Pilot, 10 Units

**Goal:** Launch, validate demand, prove the 5-zone UVP, and build 10 units with the revised enclosure still inside the $1,500 budget.

## Budget Summary

- **Total cost:** $1,494.20
- **Personal spend needed (after 1 pre-order):** $1,245.20
- **Safety net:** $254.80
- **Revenue:** $2,490
- **Profit:** $995.80

## Pricing

- **$249 flat**
- No discounts
- No free shipping — charge shipping separately if needed to protect margin

## Pre-order Strategy

Collect a minimum of **1 pre-order at $249** before ordering any parts. Total batch cost is still under the $1,500 ceiling by **$5.80**, and one pre-order increases the available safety buffer to **$254.80**.

## Build Sequence

1. Collect 1 pre-order
2. Place single combined AliExpress/LCSC order
3. Place Amazon domestic order
4. Order ABS sheet from Inventables
5. Confirm school printer access + buy 1 spool ABS filament
6. Get Pumping Station One membership ($50)
7. Print centre platforms at school (6–8 hrs each, run in parallel if possible)
8. Laser cut all panels at Pumping Station One
9. Finish 3D printed parts (sand → acetone → prime → paint → clear coat)
10. Assemble units (45–60 min each)
11. QC each unit
12. Pack and ship as orders come in

## Batch 2 Trigger

After all 10 units are sold and customer feedback is collected. Do not start Batch 2 planning until Batch 1 is fully sold through.
