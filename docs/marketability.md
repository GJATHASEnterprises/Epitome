# Step — Market Positioning

---

## Target Buyer

**Primary:** iPhone + Apple Watch + AirPods user who spends money on their desk setup.

They have a nice keyboard, a monitor arm, a desk lamp they paid too much for. They notice when something doesn't look right. They've looked at Anker and Belkin but both felt generic.

**Secondary:** Any Android/Qi phone + earbuds + watch user who wants a clean desk solution.

**Context:** Desk or nightstand. Replaces the pile of chargers and cables they already have. Makes their charging situation look intentional.

---

| Product | Price | Design | Weakness |

| **Step** | **$89** | **3-step staircase, walnut** | — |

---

## Differentiators

### 1. Stepped geometry — nobody else does this
Every other 3-in-1 charger is flat or uses arms. Step is a staircase. You can see all three devices at a glance. It reads differently in a photo. It looks like a designed object, not a tech accessory.

### 2. Walnut
Real oiled walnut surfaces. The same wood buyers already associate with Grovemade, Studio Neat, and premium desk accessories. ABS plastic can't compete at this visual level.

### 3. Two real USB-C ports
60W and 30W on the rear. Most 3-in-1 chargers have zero wired ports. Step adds meaningful utility without cluttering the front face.

### 4. Handmade, small batch
Not manufactured. Each unit is hand-assembled at a school makerspace in Chicago. That story sells to the buyer who's already spending money on their desk.

### 5. Price positioning
$89 undercuts Belkin ($130) while being premium enough not to look cheap. At $89 you compete on design, not on cost.

## Sales Channels

### Phase 1 — Find 10 buyers
1. **Reddit:** r/malelivingspace, r/homeoffice, r/mechanicalkeyboards. Post one photo. Short copy. No sales language.
2. **Etsy:** Handmade desk accessories sell well at $70–250. "Walnut wireless charging stand" is a real search term.
3. **Instagram:** One hero photo. Tag desk setup accounts. No paid ads in Batch 1.

### Phase 2 — Validate and scale
4. **Shopify (epitomecharge.com):** Direct sales, full margin, control the story.
5. **Grovemade marketplace:** If Grovemade accepts third-party products — this fits their aesthetic exactly.

## Photo Strategy

**One hero photo. This is everything.**

Shot: wooden desk, monitor, mechanical keyboard, plant. Step in corner. iPhone on Zone 1, Apple Watch on Zone 3, AirPods on Zone 2. All three LEDs gently glowing. Walnut grain visible. No clutter.

This photo needs to make someone stop scrolling and think: "what is that, I need it."

No product-on-white. No tech press release aesthetic. Desk context photo only.

---

## Why $89 Works

- Below $100 psychological threshold
- Undercuts Belkin by $41
- Covers costs with margin at just 8 units
- Premium enough that buyers don't think "cheap gadget"
- Leaves room to raise price in Batch 2 if demand is there
