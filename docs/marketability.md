## Key Selling Points

1. **True universal compatibility:** all Qi phones, all USB-C laptops/tablets, all Qi watches.
2. **Captive slot cables:** device slides in on thin edge, cable hangs from top of slot — natural plug-in on insertion.
3. **Compact footprint:** ~250mm wide (about the size of a large book) — fits any desk.
4. **3-step charging theatre:** tapered centre platform with dedicated phone, buds, and watch stages.
5. **Zone 3 dual-mode watch charging:** Apple puck plus Qi coil, no accessory swap.
6. **Thermal-aware architecture:** PSU moved under the centre platform, with the slot walls and rear spine keeping the Qi zones structurally and thermally separated.

---

## Physical Features

- ~250mm wide (compact footprint — about the size of a large book)
- Laptop slot 35mm wide / 400mm long, device on thin edge, cable from top
- Tablet slot 20mm wide / 290mm long, device on thin edge, cable from top
- Captive cable hangs from top of each slot for natural plug-in on device insertion
- 3-step tapered centre stack (180/140/100 widths) raised over the PSU cavity for a 95mm overall centre-column height
- Step riser reinforcement (internal ribbing on each riser)

## Multi-Device Charging Callout

| Zone | Method | Max Power |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Zone 1 | Qi2 wireless | 20W |
| Zone 2 | Qi wireless (120×80mm dish — buds or second phone) | 20W |
| Zone 3 | Watch puck + Qi (one active) | 5W |
| Zone 4 | USB-C PD captive cable | 100W |
| Zone 5 | USB-C PD captive cable | 45W |

**Total draw at full load: 190W** (**201W PSU Mean Well LRS-200-24, 11W headroom**; 185W ATtiny85 soft cap).

## USB-C PD Universal Compatibility Callout

- Works with any modern USB-C laptop or tablet.
- Captive cable design removes fixed-port alignment risk.
- For >100W laptops, charging continues at up to 100W with normal reduced peak replenishment.
