# Epitome Step — Market Positioning (Sept 2026 planning estimates)

## SKU narrative

| Model | Price | Buyer | Why it converts |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Step Walnut | $99 | Minimalist / wood-desk setups | Warm material story + clean status light |
| Step Obsidian | $109 | Gamer / battlestation setups | Matte carbon + RGB glow-line identity |

## Economics fit

- Walnut net: ~ $30–32/sale
- Obsidian net: ~ $34–38/sale
- Shipping is customer-paid; checkout clarity is part of conversion strategy.

## Message strategy

- Walnut copy: “minimal, warm, desk-first”.
- Obsidian copy: “dark battlestation, configurable RGB glow”.
- Technical trust language:
  - “Qi2 magnetic charging — compatible with MagSafe-case iPhones and Qi2 Android phones.”
  - “Up to 60W total.”
  - “Buds or second phone on Zone 2 (up to 15W, non-magnetic).”

## Watch-zone honesty requirement

- Generic watch coils do not charge Apple Watch.
- Do not depict Apple Watch charging unless an MFi module is selected.
