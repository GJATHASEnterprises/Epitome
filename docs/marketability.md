## Key Selling Points

1. **Charges 3 phones simultaneously** — Zone 1 (15W Qi), Zone 2 (15W Qi), Zone 4 (up to 27W USB-C). No other dock at this price does this.
2. **Charges any modern laptop** — USB-C PD 100W on Zone 4, works with any laptop from the last 6 years. MacBook Pro 16", Dell XPS, HP Spectre, Lenovo ThinkPad, ASUS, Surface Pro, and more.
3. **Built-in Apple Watch puck** — no adapter or cable required. Sculpted teardrop cradle at 30° tilt.
4. **Smart app** — theft alerts, ambient auto-dim, BLE proximity detection, weekly charge reports, home screen widget.
5. **LED zone indicators** — frosted glow bar with laser-etched icons (📱 PHONE, 🎧 BUDS, ⌚ WATCH, 💻 LAPTOP). Red = charging, Green = full, Off = empty. No guessing.
6. **Arc enclosure** — brushed aluminum top plate, soft-touch ABS base, smooth curved wedge with zero sharp corners. Looks and feels premium.
7. **No power brick** — plugs directly into the wall via IEC C13. No wall wart, no clutter.
8. **Vertical laptop storage** — laptop stands on its spine in the rear-right groove. Clean, stable, no dedicated stand required.
9. **One product, two colors** — same quality in both. No confusing tier structure.
10. **Works with everything** — Qi universal standard, USB-C PD universal — not locked to Apple-only.

- Sculpted watch cradle — teardrop shape, 30° tilt toward user, rear-left position
- Laptop groove — rear-right, 22mm wide × 12mm deep, silicone-lined, laptop stands vertically on spine
- Frosted LED diffuser strip under front lip overhang — indirect soft glow
- LED bar divided into 4 sections with recessed divider lines
- Laser-etched zone icons on aluminum top plate above each LED section
- Quad-Dock wordmark laser-etched on rear of aluminum top plate
- Rubber feet with Quad-Dock logo embossed
- Snap-fit base + 2 M3 screws only
- Cooling vents on base
- Removable top plate for service access

## 3-Phone Charging Callout

Quad-Dock can charge **3 phones at the same time**:

| Zone | Method | Max Power |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Zone 1 | 15W Qi (wireless) | 15W |
| Zone 2 | 15W Qi (wireless) | 15W |
| Zone 4 | USB-C PD (cable) | up to 27W |

**Total draw for 3 phones: ~60W** — well within the 180W PSU budget.

---

## USB-C PD Universal Compatibility Callout

Zone 4 uses standard USB-C Power Delivery (PD). This means:

- ✅ Works with **any USB-C laptop** made in the last 6 years
- ✅ No proprietary connectors, no brand lock-in
- ✅ Negotiates wattage automatically — gives each device exactly what it needs
- ✅ MacBook Pro 16" charges at 100W (slightly slower under peak CPU/GPU load, which needs 140W for full speed — normal behaviour for any 100W charger)
- ✅ Phones, iPads, Nintendo Switch — anything USB-C PD works on Zone 4
