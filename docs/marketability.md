## Key Selling Points

1. **5 charging zones in one footprint** — phone, buds, watch, laptop, and a dedicated iPad/phone USB-C groove.
2. **Charges any modern laptop** — USB-C PD 100W on Zone 4, works with any laptop from the last 6 years. MacBook Pro 16", Dell XPS, HP Spectre, Lenovo ThinkPad, ASUS, Surface Pro, and more.
3. **Dedicated side-by-side iPad / phone groove** — Zone 5 adds a second USB-C charging lane at 20W without sacrificing the laptop slot.
4. **Built-in Apple Watch puck** — no adapter or cable required. Sculpted teardrop cradle at 30° tilt.
5. **Smart app** — theft alerts, ambient auto-dim, BLE proximity detection, weekly charge reports, home screen widget.
6. **Night mode** — LEDs off 23:00–07:00 unless a zone is actively charging; overnight theft alerts can be silenced by default.
7. **LED zone indicators** — frosted glow bar with laser-etched icons (📱 PHONE, 🎧 BUDS, ⌚ WATCH, 💻 LAPTOP, 📲 iPAD). Red = charging, Green = full, Off = empty. No guessing.
8. **Arc enclosure** — brushed aluminum top plate, soft-touch ABS base, smooth curved wedge with zero sharp corners. Looks and feels premium.
9. **No power brick** — plugs directly into the wall via IEC C13 and now bundles the cable in-box. No wall wart, no clutter.
10. **Warranty registration card included** — improves premium feel and captures buyer emails for batch 2 follow-up.

- Smooth curved wedge ("Arc") enclosure — wide at rear, slightly narrower at front
- Zero sharp corners — R20mm radius on all edges
- 300mm × 110mm (front) to 140mm (rear) × 12mm (front) to 22mm (rear) height
- Brushed aluminum top plate — Gunmetal (Black), Silver (White)
- Soft-touch matte ABS base — Black or White
- Silicone-lined zone pockets — flush, 2–3mm deep recessed dishes per zone
- Sculpted watch cradle — teardrop shape, 30° tilt toward user, rear-left position
- Laptop groove — right half, 22mm wide × 12mm deep, silicone-lined, laptop stands vertically on spine
- iPad/phone groove — parallel to the laptop groove, 18mm wide × 12mm deep, silicone-lined, USB-C port in groove
- Frosted LED diffuser strip under front lip overhang — indirect soft glow
- LED bar divided into 5 sections with recessed divider lines
- Laser-etched zone icons on aluminum top plate above each LED section
- Epitome Penta wordmark laser-etched on rear of aluminum top plate
- Rubber feet with Epitome Penta logo embossed
- Snap-fit base + 2 M3 screws only
- Cooling vents on base
- Removable top plate for service access

## Multi-Device Charging Callout

Epitome Penta can charge **a laptop, phone, buds/watch, and a USB-C tablet/phone at the same time**:

| Zone | Method | Max Power |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Zone 1 | 15W Qi (wireless) | 15W |
| Zone 2 | 5W Qi (wireless) | 5W |
| Zone 3 | Watch puck | 5W |
| Zone 4 | USB-C PD (laptop groove) | 100W |
| Zone 5 | USB-C PD (iPad/phone groove) | 20W |

**Total draw at full load: 145W** — within the 180W PSU budget, with 35W spare.

---

## USB-C PD Universal Compatibility Callout

Zone 4 uses standard USB-C Power Delivery (PD) for laptops, while Zone 5 handles iPad / phone duty with a dedicated 20W PD lane. This means:

- ✅ Works with **any USB-C laptop** made in the last 6 years on Zone 4
- ✅ Gives iPad / Android / iPhone 15+ users a clean second wired charging slot on Zone 5
- ✅ No proprietary connectors, no brand lock-in
- ✅ Negotiates wattage automatically — gives each device exactly what it needs
- ✅ MacBook Pro 16" charges at 100W (slightly slower under peak CPU/GPU load, which needs 140W for full speed — normal behaviour for any 100W charger)
