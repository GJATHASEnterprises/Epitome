## Contents

| Item | Qty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Epitome Step (Walnut or Obsidian) | 1 | Main unit |
| 100W USB-C GaN brick | 1 | Compact GaN, USB-C output |
| USB-C to DC barrel cable 1 m | 1 | For brick to dock power inlet |
| USB-C cable 1 m | 2 | For user devices — BYOC "starter pack" |
| Setup card | 1 | 85 × 55 mm, matte black, printed white |
| Belly band | 1 | Wraps unit + box |

### Why 3 USB-C cables?
The two bonus USB-C cables mean customers can plug in their charging setup immediately. The dock has two USB-C ports — one cable for each. The third cable is the power cable from brick to dock barrel adapter. Everything needed is in the box.
