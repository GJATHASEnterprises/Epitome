## Box Spec

- **Type:** Magnetic closure rigid box (lift-off lid)
- **Size:** ~300×160×130mm
- **Colour:** Matte black
- **Foam:** Black foam — NOT white foam. White foam looks budget. Black foam reads premium.
- **Foam insert 1:** Dock-shaped cutout holds the dock in position
- **Foam insert 2:** Separate foam block for IEC cable + velcro tie

## Velcro Cable Tie

- Matte black

## Why Black Foam (Not White)

The magnetic closure matte black rigid box with black foam inserts reads as a premium product. White foam looks like generic electronics packaging. The extra cost difference is negligible — black foam die-cut inserts are only marginally more expensive than white.
