- **Exterior:** Rigid matte black, tuck-top
- **Interior:** Black foam insert, custom die-cut
- **Dimensions:** Sized to Step footprint + accessories (~200×130×100mm)

---

## What's NOT Included

- USB-C cables for Port A and Port B — BYOC (user brings their own)
- USB-A cables or adapters

This is by design. Users already have the cables they prefer. Not including cables reduces cost and reduces packaging waste.
